Questions

Before you start

Is Anuva Wellness right for me?

Yes, if you’re experiencing symptoms like irregular periods, hot flashes, mood changes, poor sleep, fatigue, or brain fog. Whether you’re just starting perimenopause or already navigating menopause, ANUVA WELLNESS helps you understand your symptoms and supports you with personalised guidance.

How does the 14-day free trial work?

You get full access to the assessment, daily tracking, insights and Anu for fourteen days at no cost. At the end of the fourteen days you choose whether to subscribe for ₹1,999 for 6 months, which unlocks your personalised report, your care plan and your four consultations. If you do nothing, you are not charged.

What exactly do I get for ₹1,999 for 6 months?

A personalised health report, an expert-built care plan covering nutrition, movement and lifestyle, four 1:1 expert consultations, access to Anu, daily symptom tracking, weekly, monthly and quarterly progress reports, and the member community with expert Q&A.

Are the consultations with licensed professionals?

Yes. Consultations are 1:1 sessions with practising gynaecologists, psychologists, nutritionists and menopause coaches. You can spend your four free consultations across any combination of those four disciplines depending on what you need most.

Can Anu replace my doctor?

No, and it is not meant to. Anu explains your tracking, walks you through your care plan and answers questions at any hour. It does not diagnose conditions and it does not prescribe medication or hormone therapy. When something needs a clinician, Anu says so and your consultations are there for exactly that.

Who can see my health data?

You and the expert with whom you shared it, for the purpose of that consultation. Your data is encrypted. You can export or delete it at any time under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. The details are in our privacy policy.

When will I start seeing results?

Most women start seeing usable patterns in their own tracking within the first few weeks. Changes to symptoms depend on what is driving them and on the plan you are given, so this is not a promise of a timeline. Your weekly reports will show you honestly whether things are moving.

Is this a medical service?

Anuva is a wellness and care navigation platform with teleconsultation built in. The guidance, tracking and reports are educational and supportive. The consultations are real clinical appointments with licensed practitioners. Anuva does not provide emergency care. If you are in an emergency, contact your doctor or your nearest hospital.
