Questions

Before you start

Is Anuva Wellness right for me?

If you are having irregular periods, hot flashes, mood changes, poor sleep, fatigue or brain fog, yes. You do not need a diagnosis and your periods do not need to have stopped. Anuva is built for the years leading up to menopause as well as the years after it.

How does the 14-day free trial work?

You get full access to the assessment, daily tracking, insights and Anu for fourteen days at no cost. At the end of the fourteen days you choose whether to subscribe for ₹1,499 a year plus GST, which unlocks your personalized report, your care plan and your three consultations. If you do nothing, you are not charged.

What exactly do I get for ₹1,499 a year?

A personalized health report, an expert-built care plan covering nutrition, movement and lifestyle, three 1:1 expert consultations, unlimited access to Anu, daily symptom tracking, weekly and monthly progress reports, and the member community with expert Q&A. There are no add-ons and no per-consultation charges.

Are the consultations with licensed professionals?

Yes. Consultations are 1:1 video sessions with practising gynaecologists, psychologists, nutritionists and menopause coaches. You can spend your three consultations across any combination of those four disciplines depending on what you need most.

Can Anu replace my doctor?

No, and it is not meant to. Anu explains your tracking, walks you through your care plan and answers questions at any hour. It does not diagnose conditions and it does not prescribe medication or hormone therapy. When something needs a clinician, Anu says so and your consultations are there for exactly that.

Who can see my health data?

You, and the expert you book a consultation with, for the purpose of that consultation. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. You can export it or request erasure at any time under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. The details are in our privacy policy.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel during the trial and you are never charged. Cancel after subscribing and your membership runs to the end of the year you paid for, with no renewal.

When will I start seeing results?

Most women start seeing usable patterns in their own tracking within the first two weeks, which is the point of the trial. Changes to symptoms depend on what is driving them and on the plan you are given, so this is not a promise of a timeline. Your weekly reports will show you honestly whether things are moving.

Is this a medical service?

Anuva is a wellness and care navigation platform with teleconsultation built in. The guidance, tracking and reports are educational and supportive. The consultations are real clinical appointments with licensed practitioners. Anuva does not provide emergency care. If you are in an emergency, contact your doctor or your nearest hospital.
