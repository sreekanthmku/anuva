Our story

Inspired by two women.Created for millions more.

Every meaningful journey begins with a story. Ours began with two women: Helen Moraes and Gunja Sharma.

ITwo women

Through their experiences and conversations, we began to recognise something that often goes unnoticed: the silent physical and emotional changes women experience during perimenopause and menopause.

IIWhat goes unnoticed

It wasn’t just about changing hormones. It was about

  • sleepless nights,
  • unexplained emotions,
  • feeling misunderstood,
  • and families who genuinely cared but didn’t always know how to help.

IIIThe question

That realisation sparked a simple yet powerful question:

What if women didn’t have to navigate this phase of life alone?

That question became the foundation of Anuva Wellness.

IVThe journey

  1. 1
    The inspiration

    Real experiences. Real conversations.

    A need for greater understanding.

  2. 2
    The realisation

    Women need more than information.

    They need empathy, guidance, and a support system that understands what they’re going through.

  3. 3
    Our mission

    To bring women, families, and healthcare professionals together, empowering women to navigate midlife with confidence, clarity, and care.

VThe people behind Anuva Wellness

Founded by Wilfred Moraes, alongside co-founders Roger D’Souza and Sreekanth Mk, Anuva Wellness was built on one simple belief:

Supporting a woman through midlife should never be her responsibility alone.
