About Anuva Wellness

The years nobody
prepared you for

Perimenopause lasts up to a decade, touches almost everything, and most women are handed a shrug and a suggestion to wait it out. We are building the alternative: one place where the science, the experts and the day-to-day support finally sit together.

Our story

It started with a question no one would answer

A founder watched her mother spend two years being told her exhaustion, her sleeplessness and her sudden anxiety were unrelated, ordinary, and probably stress. Four doctors. No one said the word perimenopause.

That story turned out to be almost everyone's story. Symptoms get treated one at a time, by people who never speak to each other, while the thing connecting them goes unnamed for years.

So we built Anuva around the connection rather than the symptoms: a single assessment, one plan drawn from it, and a team that actually shares notes. Track what is happening, see the pattern, and get guidance that changes as you do.

Our mission

To make the second half of a woman's health story as well understood, as well supported and as unremarkable to talk about as the first.

What we believe

Three things we will not trade away

Evidence over anecdote

Every plan traces back to published research and a named clinician. If we cannot support it, we do not suggest it.

Built for Indian bodies

Indian diets, Indian family structures, Indian clinics. Not a Western protocol with the units converted.

Privacy is the product

Health data is encrypted, never sold, and yours to delete at any time. We treat the DPDP Act as a floor, not a ceiling.
