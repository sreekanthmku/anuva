Before you start

Is Anuva right for me?

If you are having irregular periods, hot flashes, mood changes, poor sleep, fatigue or brain fog, yes. You do not need a diagnosis and your periods do not need to have stopped.

Can Anu replace my doctor?

No, and it is not meant to. Anu explains your tracking, walks you through your care plan and answers questions at any hour. It does not diagnose conditions and it does not prescribe medication or hormone therapy. Your three consultations are there for exactly that.

Is the community anonymous, and who can see my data?

Questions can be asked anonymously, and nothing you log is visible to other members. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, visible to you and to the expert you book with, and you can export it or request erasure at any time under the DPDP Act, 2023. See our privacy policy.

Full access to the assessment, daily tracking, insights and Anu, at no cost. On day 14 you choose whether to subscribe. If you do nothing, nothing is taken.
