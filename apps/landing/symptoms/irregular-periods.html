Irregular Periods

What it feels like

Periods arrive earlier or later, you skip a month, or your bleeding becomes lighter or heavier than usual.

Why it happens

Changes to your periods can be an early sign of perimenopause. Ovulation becomes less predictable as ovarian hormone patterns change. Other conditions can also cause changes in bleeding.
