Who we are

Anuva Wellness (“Anuva”, “we”, “us”) operates the Anuva Wellness website and application, a perimenopause and menopause wellness service for users in India. In the language of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ↗, we are the Data Fiduciary for the personal data described here, and you are the Data Principal.

Registered entity: [Legal entity name], [registered address], [CIN / GSTIN].

What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details — name, mobile number, email address, and the one-time passwords used to sign you in.
  • Health information — your assessment answers, symptoms, cycle details, sleep, mood, weight, medical history, medications and anything you write to Anu or ask in the community. This is sensitive personal data and we treat it accordingly.
  • Consultation records — bookings and the notes an expert records during a session with you.
  • Payment details — handled by our payment gateway. We receive a transaction reference and status. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Anything you send us — messages through the contact form or by email.

Information collected automatically

  • Device type, operating system, browser and app version.
  • How you use the service: screens opened, features used, timestamps.
  • An approximate location derived from your IP address (city level, not precise).
  • Diagnostic and crash logs.

How we use it

We use your data only for the purposes you gave it to us for:

  • To generate your personalized health report and care plan.
  • To show you your own patterns in weekly and monthly reports.
  • To let Anu, our AI companion, answer your questions in context.
  • To arrange consultations and share the relevant history with the expert you booked.
  • To take payment, manage your subscription and issue invoices.
  • To answer your messages and provide support.
  • To keep the service secure, detect abuse and fix faults.
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not sell or share your health data with advertisers, insurers or employers. We do not use your health data to target advertising to you.

Consent, and the basis for processing

Under the DPDP Act we process your personal data on the basis of the consent you give when you create an account and when you complete the health assessment. That consent is asked for in plain language, is specific to the purposes listed above, and covers only the data needed for them.

You can withdraw consent at any time, from your profile settings or by writing to our Grievance Officer. Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it. Doing so stops future processing; it does not undo processing that already, lawfully, happened, and it may mean we can no longer provide parts of the service.

Where the law requires us to keep certain records (tax and accounting records, for example), we retain those for the period the law specifies even after you withdraw consent or delete your account.

Who we share it with

We share your data only with the following, and only as far as each needs it:

  • The experts you book — gynaecologists, psychologists, nutritionists and menopause coaches see the health context relevant to your consultation. They are bound by confidentiality.
  • Data Processors acting for us — cloud hosting, database, email and messaging, analytics, crash reporting, payment gateway and AI model providers. They act only on our instructions under contract, and may not use your data for their own purposes.
  • Authorities — where we are required to disclose by law, court order or a lawful government request.
  • A successor entity — if Anuva is acquired or merged, under the same commitments set out here. We will tell you before your data moves.

Some of these providers process data outside India. Where that happens we rely on contractual safeguards and transfer only to countries not restricted by the Central Government under the DPDP Act.

How we protect it

  • Encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest.
  • Access controls, so staff reach your health data only when there is a reason to.
  • Phone-based one-time passwords rather than stored passwords.
  • Logging and monitoring of access to sensitive records.
  • Periodic review of our security practices and our processors’.

No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach occurs, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India as the DPDP Act requires.

How long we keep it

  • While your account is active — we keep your data so your history and trends remain useful to you.
  • After you delete your account — we erase or irreversibly anonymise your personal and health data within 90 days, except where the law requires us to keep specific records.
  • Financial records — retained for the period required by Indian tax law.
  • Backups — deleted data persists in encrypted backups for a short rolling window before those backups expire.

Your rights

As a Data Principal under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we have processed it.
  • Correct, complete, update or erase your personal data.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated.
  • Grievance redressal — a readily available means of raising a complaint with us, which you must use before approaching the Data Protection Board.

Most of this is available directly in the app under Profile. For anything else, write to our Grievance Officer below. We respond within 30 days. You also have a duty under the Act not to raise false or frivolous complaints and to give accurate information about yourself.

Children and guardianship

Anuva is intended for adults aged 18 and over and we do not knowingly collect data from children. Where we process the data of a child or a person with a disability who has a lawful guardian, we do so only with verifiable consent from the parent or guardian, and we do not undertake tracking, behavioural monitoring or targeted advertising directed at them, as the DPDP Act requires.

Cookies and similar technologies

We use a small number of cookies and local storage keys to keep you signed in, remember your preferences and understand which features get used. We do not use third-party advertising cookies. You can clear or block them in your browser, though signing in may stop working if you block the essential ones.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change, and revise the date at the top. If a change materially affects how we handle your data, we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.

Contact
